# Classic arimaa.com artwork

Board and piece images from the original arimaa.com game client (2003),
released to the public domain. Source archive: `AllImages.zip`.

No attribution is required; this note records provenance.

Files used here are unmodified: `BoardStoneBig.jpg`, `BoardMarbleBig.jpg`
and `pieces/*.png` (74×74 RGBA).

The stone theme's step sounds (`sounds/stone/`) are made like the app's
default board sounds (`src/sounds/board/ATTRIBUTION.md`), from Kenney's
CC0 samples `card-shove-1` and `chip-lay-2` (Casino Audio) at a different
pitch; `preset.json` holds their settings.
