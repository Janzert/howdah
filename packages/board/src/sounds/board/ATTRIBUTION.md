# Board sounds

The default board sounds (`step`, `lastStep`, `capture`, `ownLoss` and
`restore`), mixed and filtered from samples in Kenney's audio packs
(kenney.nl), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication):

- `card-shove-1` and `chip-lay-2` from Casino Audio
- `minimize_008` from Interface Sounds
- `footstep_wood_001` from Impact Sounds

No attribution is required; this note records provenance. A step is the
slide sample ending in the chip as a click; the capture is the interface
sound with the wooden footstep after it; the own-piece loss is the capture
lower and duller; restore is the interface sound reversed.
`preset.json` holds the settings they were rendered from (16-bit mono
WAV, 48 kHz).
